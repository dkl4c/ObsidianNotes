> [!info] **基本初等函数的麦克劳林展开式**
> 以下为最常用的展开式，$R$ 为收敛半径：
>
> $$ \frac{1}{1-x} = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} x^n = 1 + x + x^2 + \cdots, \quad R = 1 $$
> $$ e^x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n!} = 1 + x + \frac{x^2}{2!} + \cdots, \quad R = \infty $$
> $$ \sin x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (-1)^n \frac{x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!} = x - \frac{x^3}{3!} + \frac{x^5}{5!} - \cdots, \quad R = \infty $$
> $$ \cos x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (-1)^n \frac{x^{2n}}{(2n)!} = 1 - \frac{x^2}{2!} + \frac{x^4}{4!} - \cdots, \quad R = \infty $$
> $$ \ln(1+x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^{n-1} \frac{x^n}{n} = x - \frac{x^2}{2} + \frac{x^3}{3} - \cdots, \quad R = 1 $$
> $$ (1+x)^\alpha = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \binom{\alpha}{n} x^n, \quad R = 1 $$
> 其中 $\displaystyle\binom{\alpha}{n} = \frac{\alpha(\alpha-1)\cdots(\alpha-n+1)}{n!}$。
